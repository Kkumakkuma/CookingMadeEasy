## What Most Guides Get Wrong

### Misleading Versatility Claims
While many guides exaggerate the versatility of air fryers, in my experience, not all models can perform every function perfectly. For instance, air fryers like the Ninja Foodi can roast well, but some cheaper brands struggle with baking, which I learned when my brownies turned out more like muffins.

### Overemphasis on Size
Size really does matter when it comes to air fryers, and many guides fail to mention this. I once bought a huge air fryer, thinking it would be perfect for family meals, only to find that it consumed too much counter space. In contrast, a compact model worked just fine for meal prepping.

### Misunderstood Cooking Time
Many air fryer guides point to average cooking times but don’t factor in personal preferences or food types. I often found that cooking chicken nuggets will take longer than advertised, which can lead to undercooked meals if you don’t keep a close eye.
