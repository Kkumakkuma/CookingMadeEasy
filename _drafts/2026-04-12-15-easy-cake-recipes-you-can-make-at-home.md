## 1. Classic Vanilla Cake

Nothing beats a classic vanilla cake. It's light, fluffy, and pairs beautifully with many icings or fillings.

### Ingredients:
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 ½ cups sugar
- ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup milk
- 3 ½ tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ½ tsp salt
- 3 large eggs

### Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease two 9-inch round cake pans.
2. In a large bowl, cream together the butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then stir in the vanilla.
4. Combine flour, baking powder, and salt in another bowl. Gradually add it to the creamed mixture, alternating with milk.
5. Mix until just combined. Pour the batter evenly into the prepared pans.
6. Bake for 30-35 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Let it cool before frosting.

## 2. Chocolate Cake

Rich, decadent chocolate cake is a crowd-pleaser that never goes out of style!

### Ingredients:
- 1 ¾ cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups sugar
- ¾ cup cocoa powder
- 1 ½ tsp baking soda
- 1 ½ tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup milk
- ½ cup vegetable oil
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup boiling water

### Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease two 9-inch round cake pans.
2. In a large bowl, mix the dry ingredients together.
3. Add the eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla to the dry mix and beat until well combined.
4. Carefully stir in the boiling water (batter will be thin) and mix until smooth.
5. Pour into prepared pans and bake for 30-35 minutes. Cool before icing with your favorite chocolate frosting.

## 3. Lemon Drizzle Cake

This zesty lemon drizzle cake is perfect for those who love a bright, refreshing flavor.

### Ingredients:
- 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup sugar
- ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
- 2 large eggs
- ½ cup milk
- 1 ½ tsp baking powder
- Zest of 2 lemons
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- For the drizzle: 1 cup powdered sugar and 3 tbsp lemon juice

### Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a loaf pan.
2. In a bowl, cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Add eggs one at a time.
3. Mix in the lemon zest, juice, and milk.
4. Gradually add flour and baking powder until just combined.
5. Pour into the loaf pan and bake for 50-60 minutes. 
6. For the drizzle, mix the powdered sugar with lemon juice, then pour over the cake when it’s cooled.

## 4. Red Velvet Cake

This show-stopping cake with its vibrant color is sure to impress!

### Ingredients:
- 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 ½ cups sugar
- 1 cup vegetable oil
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tbsp cocoa powder
- 1 ½ tbsp red food coloring
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp vinegar

### Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease two 9-inch round cake pans.
2. In a large bowl, mix the oil, and sugar. Add eggs, buttermilk, and vanilla.
3. In another bowl, combine flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt, then mix into the wet ingredients.
4. Add food coloring and vinegar, stirring until fully combined.
5. Divide the batter between pans and bake for 25-30 minutes. Cool completely before frosting with cream cheese icing.

## 5. Carrot Cake

This moist cake is dotted with bits of carrot, making it a perfect dessert that’s also a bit healthy!

### Ingredients:
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 cup vegetable oil
- 4 large eggs
- 3 cups grated carrots
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp salt 

### Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x13 inch pan.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon.
3. In separate bowl, mix the oil, eggs, and grated carrots. Add this to the dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
4. Pour into the prepared pan and bake for 40-45 minutes. Cool before frosting with cream cheese frosting.

## 6. Coconut Cake

Coconut lovers, this cake is for you! It's moist, fluffy, and topped with shredded coconut.

### Ingredients:
- 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 ½ cups sugar
- ¾ cup unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup coconut milk
- 3 large eggs
- 1 ½ tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup shredded coconut (sweetened or unsweetened)

### Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease two 8-inch round cake pans.
2. Cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Add eggs and vanilla.
3. Mix flour and baking powder and add it alternately with coconut milk to the creamed mixture.
4. Fold in the shredded coconut.
5. Pour batter into pans and bake for 25-30 minutes. Cool and frost with whipped cream and additional coconut.

## 7. Funfetti Cake

Perfect for birthdays, this colorful cake brings fun to any celebration!

### Ingredients:
- 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 ½ cups sugar
- ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup milk
- 3 large eggs
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ cup rainbow sprinkles

### Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease two 9-inch round cake pans.
2. In a large bowl, cream the butter and sugar until fluffy. Beat in eggs.
3. Mix flour, baking powder, and salt in another bowl, then gradually add to the creamy mixture with milk.
4. Carefully fold in the sprinkles.
5. Divided the batter into the prepared pans and bake for 30-35 minutes. Cool and frost as desired.

## 8. Marble Cake

Can’t decide between chocolate and vanilla? Have both with a marble cake!

### Ingredients:
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 ½ cups sugar
- ¾ cup unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup milk
- 3 large eggs
- 2 tsp baking powder
- ½ cup cocoa powder (for the chocolate part)

### Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9-inch round cake pan.
2. Cream the butter and sugar together, adding eggs one at a time. 
3. Mix in the flour and baking powder, alternating with milk.
4. Divide the batter into two bowls. Mix cocoa powder into one half.
5. Pour vanilla and chocolate batters alternately into the pan and swirl with a knife.
6. Bake for 30-35 minutes. Cool before icing your favorite frosting.

## 9. Banana Cake

A delightful way to use overripe bananas, this cake is soft and flavorful!

### Ingredients:
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup sugar
- ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup mashed bananas (about 2 large)
- 2 large eggs
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

### Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and lightly grease a 9-inch round pan.
2. Cream together the butter and sugar until fluffy. Add eggs and mix well.
3. Stir in mashed bananas and vanilla.
4. Combine flour, baking soda, and salt in a separate bowl. Gradually add to the banana mixture.
5. Pour into the pan and bake for 30-35 minutes. Cool and frost with cream cheese frosting for extra deliciousness.

## 10. Spice Cake

Warm and fragrant, this spice cake is perfect for cozy gatherings.

### Ingredients:

- 2 cups sugar
- 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup milk
- 3 large eggs
- 2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp nutmeg
- ½ tsp salt 

### Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease two 9-inch round cake pans.
2. Cream together the butter and sugar until fluffy. Add eggs one at a time.
3. Mix flour with baking soda, baking powder, salt, and spices. Add this to the creamed mixture, alternating with milk.

## 11. Almond Cake

This light almond cake is an elegant option for any gathering.

### Ingredients:
- 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup sugar
- ¾ cup unsalted butter, softened
- 3 large eggs
- ½ cup milk
- 1 tsp almond extract
- 2 tsp baking powder
- ¼ tsp salt

### Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9-inch round cake pan.
2. Cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Add eggs one at a time, mixing well.
3. Stir in the milk and almond extract.
4. Combine flour, baking powder, and salt, and mix until just combined.
5. Pour into the prepared pan and bake for 30-35 minutes. Cool before frosting with almond-flavored buttercream.

## 12. Zucchini Cake

A moist cake that hides veggies? Yes, please! This zucchini cake is delightful and perfect for summer gatherings.

### Ingredients:
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 cup vegetable oil
- 3 large eggs
- 2 cups grated zucchini
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt

### Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x13 inch pan.
2. In a mixing bowl, beat together oil and sugar. Add eggs and zucchini, mixing well.
3. Sift together flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt, and cinnamon. Combine this with the wet mixture.
4. Bake for 40-45 minutes. Allow to cool and top with cream cheese frosting.

## 13. Gingerbread Cake

Perfect for the holiday season, this cake is spicy and comforting.

### Ingredients:
- 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tbsp ground ginger
- 1 tbsp cinnamon
- ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup molasses
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup hot water

### Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x13 inch pan.
2. Cream butter and sugar. Beat in eggs, then mix in molasses.
3. In another bowl, combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, and spices.
4. Alternately add the flour mixture and hot water to the molasses mixture, mixing well.
5. Pour into prepared pan and bake for 30-35 minutes. Cool and serve with whipped cream.

## 14. Pumpkin Cake

Fall flavors come alive in this deliciously spiced pumpkin cake that everyone will love.

### Ingredients:
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 cup vegetable oil
- 1 can (15 oz) pumpkin puree
- 4 large eggs
- 2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp cinnamon

### Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x13 inch pan.
2. Mix together sugar, oil, and pumpkin. Add eggs, one at a time.
3. In another bowl, combine flour, baking soda, baking powder, and cinnamon.
4. Mix dry ingredients into wet until just combined. Bake for 30-35 minutes. Frost with cream cheese frosting for a delightful finishing touch!

## 15. Tres Leches Cake

This Latin American dessert is soaked in three types of milk, making it incredibly moist and indulgent!

### Ingredients:
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 ½ cups sugar
- ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup milk
- 5 large eggs
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk
- 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
- 1 cup heavy cream

### Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x13 inch pan.
2. Cream the butter and sugar until fluffy. Add eggs one at a time, mixing well.
3. Alternately add flour combined with baking powder and milk.
4. Bake for 30-35 minutes. Once cool, poke holes in the cake and pour the milk mixture (evaporated and condensed) over the top to soak.
5. Top with whipped cream and enjoy!
