## Key Features to Look for in Meal Prep Containers
When it comes to meal prep containers, not all are created equal. Here are essential features to consider:

### Material Matters
- **Glass Containers**: Often more durable and microwave-safe. They're ideal for those who prioritize longevity and safety. 
- **Plastic Containers**: Lightweight and usually cheaper. However, ensure they are BPA-free for safe food storage.
- **Stainless Steel**: Extremely durable and non-reactive. Best for sustainability and long-term use.

### Size Variety
- **Portions**: Some brands offer smaller sizes suitable for snacks or sides, while others focus on larger meal portions. Depending on your meal plans, consider containers that provide variety.
- **Stackable Design**: This is critical for saving space, particularly if you have limited fridge or freezer space.

### Airtight Sealing
- **Lids**: Look for containers with airtight and leak-proof lids to keep food fresh longer. Rubber seals or silicone gaskets add to this advantage.

### Microwave and Dishwasher Safety
- **Versatility**: Ensure that the containers are both microwave and dishwasher safe for convenience.

## What Most Guides Get Wrong
### Insight 1: Assume All Plastic is Equal
Many guides suggest BPA-free plastic is the sole guideline for safety.  
**Why this matters**: I learned that not all plastic also provides adequate leak resistance. A cheaper container may still leach harmful substances under heat. For example, my Utopia Kitchen containers, while affordable, were flimsy and showed degradation after just two months of microwaving.

### Insight 2: Glass is Always Superior  
It's a common misconception that glass containers are universally the best option.  
**Why this matters**: In my tests, certain high-grade plastic containers held up better against heavy usage without breaking. My GlassLock containers are great for microwave use, but they weigh significantly more and can shatter.

### Insight 3: Size Doesn't Matter as Much as Functionality  
Many people focus solely on the size of the meal prep containers.  
**Why this matters**: I have a set of smaller containers that I use far more often than larger ones. For example, the Utopia Kitchen containers became my go-to for smaller portions like snacks and sauces.

## My Favorite Recipe for Meal Prepping
Finding the right containers is only half the battle; what you put in them is equally essential. Over the years, I’ve developed a go-to meal prep recipe that I rely on weekly: **Mediterranean Chickpea Salad**. 

### Ingredients
- 1 can of chickpeas, drained ($1)
- 1 cucumber, diced ($0.75)
- 1 bell pepper, chopped ($1)
- 1/2 red onion, diced ($0.50)
- A handful of cherry tomatoes, halved ($2)
- 1/4 cup of feta cheese, crumbled ($3)
- 2 tablespoons of olive oil ($0.50)
- 1 tablespoon of lemon juice ($0.25)
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Cost Breakdown
Total cost for ingredients: **$9**  
This salad not only costs less than **$2 per serving** but is also packed with nutrients and flavors.

### Preparation Steps
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine chickpeas, cucumber, bell pepper, red onion, and cherry tomatoes.
2. Drizzle olive oil and lemon juice over the salad, then sprinkle with salt and pepper.
3. Gently mix everything until the ingredients are well combined.
4. Portion the salad into the **Prep Naturals containers**. The airtight lids keep it fresh for up to five days in the fridge.

### Benefits of This Meal
This Mediterranean Chickpea Salad is not only delicious but also offers versatility. I often pair it with grilled chicken or quinoa for a more substantial meal. The colorful ingredients make it visually appealing, and I love how it stays fresh in my containers without any wilted greens—thanks to the design and airtight seals.
