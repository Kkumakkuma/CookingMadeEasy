## Why Korean Cooking?

Korean food is known for its vibrant salads, savory pancakes, tender stews, and famous side dishes called banchan. What makes Korean cooking unique is its balance of flavors—spicy, sweet, salty, and sour—all coming together to create a harmony that delights the senses. Plus, many Korean dishes are simple to prepare, relying on fresh, wholesome ingredients. So, if you’re ready to explore the world of kimchi, bulgogi, and more, let’s dive into these ten recipes that will have you cooking like a pro!

## 1. Kimchi (김치)

No exploration of Korean cuisine is complete without kimchi, a fermented vegetable dish that’s synonymous with the country's culinary identity.

### Ingredients:
- 1 medium Napa cabbage
- 1/4 cup sea salt
- Water for soaking
- 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
- 1 tablespoon garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons fish sauce (or soy sauce for a vegetarian version)
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 2-3 tablespoons Korean red pepper flakes (gochugaru)
- 4 green onions, chopped
- 1 carrot, julienned (optional)

### Instructions:
1. **Prepare the Cabbage:** Cut the Napa cabbage lengthwise into quarters. Dissolve the sea salt in water and soak the cabbage in the salted water for about 2 hours, turning occasionally.
2. **Make the Paste:** In a bowl, mix the garlic, ginger, fish sauce, sugar, and gochugaru until it forms a paste.
3. **Rinse & Drain:** After the cabbage has soaked, rinse it thoroughly under cold water to remove excess salt and drain well.
4. **Combine Ingredients:** In a large mixing bowl, combine the cabbage, paste, green onions, and carrots (if using). Mix well with your hands, wearing gloves if desired.
5. **Pack & Ferment:** Pack the mixture tightly into a jar, leaving some space for the kimchi to expand as it ferments. Seal and let it sit at room temperature for 1-5 days, then refrigerate.

### Flavor Notes:
The crunchy texture combined with the tangy, spicy kick makes kimchi an addictive side dish or condiment.

## 2. Bibimbap (비빔밥)

Bibimbap is a colorful mixed rice dish topped with an assortment of vegetables, meat, and a fried egg, all drizzled with spicy gochujang sauce.

### Ingredients:
- 2 cups cooked rice (preferably short-grain)
- 1 cup spinach, blanched and chopped
- 1 zucchini, julienned
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 1 cup bean sprouts, blanched
- 1 cup mushrooms, sliced (shiitake or button)
- 2 eggs
- 2 tablespoons sesame oil
- 4 tablespoons gochujang (Korean red chili paste)
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions:
1. **Sauté Vegetables:** Heat a pan over medium heat. Sauté each vegetable separately in sesame oil until cooked. Season each with a pinch of salt.
2. **Fry Eggs:** In the same pan, fry the eggs sunny-side up, cooking until the whites are set but the yolks remain runny.
3. **Assemble:** In a large bowl, place the cooked rice at the bottom. Arrange each sautéed vegetable on top, along with the fried egg in the center. 
4. **Season:** Serve with a dollop of gochujang on top, and give a sprinkle of sesame seeds if desired.

### Flavor Notes:
The mix of textures and flavors in bibimbap—from the crunchy vegetables to the creamy yolk and spicy paste—creates a satisfying meal.

## 3. Bulgogi (불고기)

Sweet and savory marinated beef, bulgogi is a must-try that’s great grilled or pan-fried.

### Ingredients:
- 1 lb beef (sirloin or ribeye), thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 green onions, chopped
- Sesame seeds for garnish

### Instructions:
1. **Marinate Beef:** In a bowl, mix soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and black pepper. Add the beef and let it marinate for at least 30 minutes, or overnight for best results.
2. **Sauté:** Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the marinated beef along with the onions. Cook until the beef is browned and cooked through, about 5-7 minutes.
3. **Serve:** Garnish with sesame seeds and chopped green onions. Serve hot with rice.

### Flavor Notes:
Bulgogi has a beautiful caramelization that gives it a sweet and savory flavor profile. It's tender, juicy, and downright delicious.

## 4. Japchae (잡채)

Japchae is a flavorful stir-fried noodle dish made with sweet potato noodles and a variety of vegetables.

### Ingredients:
- 8 oz sweet potato noodles
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
- 1/2 cup spinach
- 1 onion, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 tablespoon sesame seeds

### Instructions:
1. **Cook Noodles:** Cook the sweet potato noodles according to package instructions. Drain and rinse with cold water.
2. **Sauté Veggies:** In a large skillet, heat sesame oil over medium heat. Add garlic and onion, sauté until fragrant. Then add carrots, bell pepper, mushrooms, and spinach, cooking until tender.
3. **Combine:** Add the noodles to the skillet. Pour in soy sauce and sugar, and toss everything together until well combined. Adjust seasoning as needed.
4. **Serve:** Garnish with sesame seeds before serving.

### Flavor Notes:
Japchae is a delightful medley of chewy textures and savory flavors, perfect as a side dish or a main.

## 5. Tteokbokki (떡볶이)

These spicy rice cakes are a popular street food in Korea, offering a delightful chewy texture with a kick from gochujang.

### Ingredients:
- 1 lb Korean rice cakes
- 2 cups water
- 3 tablespoons gochujang
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 green onions, chopped
- 1 boiled egg (optional)

### Instructions:
1. **Prepare Sauce:** In a pot, combine water, gochujang, sugar, and soy sauce. Stir until well combined.
2. **Cook Rice Cakes:** Add the rice cakes to the sauce and cook over medium heat for about 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally until the sauce thickens and the cakes are heated through.
3. **Add Vegetables:** Stir in the onion and cook until softened. Add the green onions just before serving.
4. **Serve:** Add a boiled egg, if desired, and serve hot.

### Flavor Notes:
The sticky, spicy sauce coats the chewy rice cakes perfectly, creating an irresistible street food experience.

## 6. Korean Fried Chicken (치킨)

Forget takeout! Korean fried chicken is extra crispy and tossed in a sticky sauce that’s hard to resist.

### Ingredients:
- 2 lbs chicken wings or drumsticks
- 1 cup potato starch (or cornstarch)
- Vegetable oil for frying
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 tablespoon gochujang
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- Sesame seeds for garnish

### Instructions:
1. **Coat Chicken:** Pat the chicken dry and coat it evenly with potato starch.
2. **Fry Chicken:** In a deep pot, heat oil to 350°F (175°C). Fry the chicken in batches until golden brown and crispy, about 8-10 minutes. Drain on paper towels.
3. **Make Sauce:** In a bowl, combine soy sauce, sugar, gochujang, garlic, ginger, and sesame oil. Heat in a pan until sugar dissolves.
4. **Coat Chicken:** Toss the fried chicken in the sauce until well coated. Garnish with sesame seeds before serving.

### Flavor Notes:
Korean fried chicken is known for its crunch and sticky sweetness, making it a perfect appetizer or main dish.

## 7. Galbi (갈비)

Tender, marinated short ribs that are either grilled or braised, galbi is a must-have favorite for many.

### Ingredients:
- 2 lbs beef short ribs
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 1/4 cup sesame oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 cup chopped green onions

### Instructions:
1. **Marinate Ribs:** In a large bowl, combine soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and pepper. Add the short ribs and marinate for at least 4 hours or overnight in the fridge.
2. **Cook the Ribs:** Grill or braise the ribs on a low flame for about 20-30 minutes, until they’re tender and juicy.
3. **Serve:** Garnish with green onions and serve hot.

### Flavor Notes:
The sweet and savory marinade seeps into the tender meat, making galbi a true treat.

## 8. Sundubu-jjigae (순두부찌개)

This spicy tofu stew is comforting and packed with flavor, making it perfect for cold nights.

### Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1-2 tablespoons gochugaru (adjust to taste)
- 4 cups vegetable or chicken broth
- 1 package silken tofu, cut into cubes
- 1/2 cup zucchini, diced
- 1 cup spinach or bok choy
- 1 egg (optional)
- Green onions for garnish

### Instructions:
1. **Sauté Aromatics:** Heat oil in a pot and sauté onion and garlic until fragrant. Add gochugaru and stir for another minute.
2. **Add Broth:** Pour in the broth and bring to a boil, then add the zucchini and cook for about 5 minutes.
3. **Incorporate Tofu & Veggies:** Add silken tofu and spinach, simmer for 10 minutes. If desired, crack an egg into the pot and let it cook for a couple of minutes.
4. **Serve:** Garnish with green onions and serve hot with rice.

### Flavor Notes:
This hearty stew has a rich, spicy broth that pairs beautifully with the soft tofu, making each bite comforting.

## 9. Kimbap (김밥)

Often described as the Korean version of sushi, kimbap consists of rice and various fillings rolled in seaweed.

### Ingredients:
- 2 cups cooked short-grain rice
- 3 tablespoons sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 4 sheets of nori (seaweed)
- 1 cucumber, julienned
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 4 strips of cooked beef or chicken
- 2 eggs, beaten and cooked into a thin omelette, then sliced

### Instructions:
1. **Season Rice:** Mix the cooked rice with sesame oil and salt.
2. **Prepare Fillings:** Have all your fillings ready in separate sections.
3. **Roll Kimbap:** Place a sheet of nori on a bamboo mat, spread a layer of rice across it, and add fillings along one side. Roll tightly and slice into bite-sized pieces.
4. **Serve:** Enjoy as a snack or lunch with pickled radish on the side.

### Flavor Notes:
Kimbap is portable, vibrant, and balanced, with a satisfying crunch from the vegetables and a hint of nuttiness from the sesame oil.

## 10. Banchan (반찬)

Korean meals often include an array of banchan, small side dishes that complement the main course. Here are a few simple ideas:

### Common Banchan Ideas:
- **Pickled Radish (Danmuji):** Sliced yellow pickled radish.
- **Seasoned Spinach (Sigeumchi Namul):** Blanched spinach seasoned with sesame oil and garlic.
- **Cucumber Salad (Oi Muchim):** Spicy cucumber salad with gochugaru, garlic, and sesame seeds.

### Instructions for Oi Muchim:
1. **Prepare Cucumber:** Slice cucumbers into thin rounds.
2. **Mix Seasoning:** In a bowl, combine gochugaru, vinegar, sugar, minced garlic, and sesame seeds.
3. **Toss Together:** Add cucumbers and toss well until evenly coated.

### Flavor Notes:
Banchan are designed to be shared, and they provide a delightful variety to any meal, brightening the table with their colorful presentation.
