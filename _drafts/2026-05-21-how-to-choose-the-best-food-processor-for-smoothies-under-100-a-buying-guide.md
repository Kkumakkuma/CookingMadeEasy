## What to Look for in a Food Processor for Smoothies
### How does a food processor work for making smoothies?
Food processors blend ingredient particles into a uniform mixture by using sharp blades that rotate at high speeds. Depending on the model, some processors can chop and puree ingredients quicker than blenders due to their design. For smoothies, aim for a device that can efficiently handle fibrous ingredients such as leafy greens and frozen fruits, which are essential for a nutritious smoothie.

### Essential Features to Consider
1. **Motor Power** - Look for at least 350 watts to handle tough ingredients.
2. **Capacity** - A capacity of at least 6 cups will allow you to make multiple servings at once.
3. **Quality Blades** - Stainless steel blades are preferred for durability and efficiency.
4. **Ease of Cleaning** - Dishwasher-safe parts can save you time and effort.
5. **Portability** - If you plan on moving your food processor often, consider its weight and the space it occupies.

2. **Not All Wattage Is Equal** - Some guides emphasize wattage without considering efficiency.
