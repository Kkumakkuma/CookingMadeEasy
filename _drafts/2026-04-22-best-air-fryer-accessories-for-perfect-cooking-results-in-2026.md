## Why Air Fryer Accessories Matter

Using an air fryer without accessories is like playing a video game without the joystick—sure, you can make it work, but you won't enjoy the experience or get the best results.

### Enhance Versatility

Adding accessories like silicone molds or baking pans allows for a wider variety of dishes, from baking bread to reheating leftovers efficiently. In my experience, I’ve doubled my air fryer usage just by incorporating these tools into my routine.

### Improve Cooking Results

Certain accessories help distribute heat evenly, ensuring that your food cooks perfectly. Using a quality air fryer rack, for instance, allowed me to make perfectly crispy fries every time. It’s all about that even airflow!

### Easy Cleanup

Most air fryer accessories are dishwasher-safe, which saves me valuable time in the kitchen. I’ve found that silicone mats or liners help avoid sticky situations and minimize residue on the fryer itself.

## What Most Guides Get Wrong

While many guides mention the basics, here are three insights I've discovered through my own testing that others often overlook:

1. **Material Matters**: Many recommend any silicone mold, but I’ve found that high-temperature silicone (like from Kitzini) is necessary if you want longevity and reduced risk of toxins leaching into your food.

2. **Don’t Underestimate Size**: A common oversight is not ensuring that your accessories fit properly in the fryer. For example, the COSORI Rack fits better in square models; be sure to measure!

3. **Dual-Purpose Accessories**: Some accessories serve multiple functions. My air fryer rack from Maxi-Matic not only saves space but can also function as a cooling rack for baked goods—it's perfect for when I'm working through recipes, as I mentioned in my post about the [Best Stand Mixers for Baking Fluffy Cakes in 2026: My Top Recommendations](/best-stand-mixers-for-baking-fluffy-cakes-in-2026-my-top-recommendations/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### ### What accessories do I really need for my air fryer?

Essential accessories include silicone baking mats, air fryer liners, and racks. Depending on your cooking style, silicone molds and skewers may also be beneficial.

### ### Are air fryer accessories expensive?

Not at all—prices generally range from $5 to $35, allowing for a variety of budget-friendly options. For current prices, check current prices on the brand's official site.

### ### Can I use regular baking pans in my air fryer?

Yes, but you need to make sure they fit properly and are rated for high temperatures. I once made the mistake of using a pan meant for conventional ovens, which did not cook well and left me frustrated.

### ### What’s the best way to clean air fryer accessories?

Most silicone and metal accessories are dishwasher safe, while others can simply be wiped down with warm, soapy water.
