A pro-purpose mixer operates significantly differently from standard kitchen mixers. It features a powerful motor and multiple speed settings specifically tailored for various tasks such as heavy dough mixing or whipping egg whites.

## What's the difference between a pro-purpose mixer and a stand mixer?

Pro-purpose mixers and stand mixers may seem similar, but they serve different purposes, tailored specifically to different baking needs.

| **Feature**             | **Pro-Purpose Mixer**                          | **Stand Mixer**                            | **Price Range**           |
|-------------------------|------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Motor Power**         | 575 watts, ideal for heavy batters and dough  | Usually 300-500 watts, best for light mixing | $349 - $629             |
| **Bowl Size**          | 6-quart to 8-quart capacities                   | 3-quart to 5-quart capacities             | $200 - $600             |
| **Durability**          | Robust, built for heavy usage                   | Lighter, designed for less intensive tasks| 5 - 10 year warranties   |
| **Versatility**         | Multiple attachments for various tasks          | Primarily for mixing and whipping         | Varies based on features  |


As you can see, the power and capacity of pro-purpose mixers truly set them apart. My experience with the KitchenAid showed its true nature when working with sticky sourdough—it could smoothly mix and knead without overheating!
