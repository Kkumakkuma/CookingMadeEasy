## How to Perfect Your Pizza Dough

My journey with the Ooni Karu 16 wasn’t just about testing the oven itself; it also led me to experiment extensively with pizza dough recipes. In the world of homemade pizzas, crust quality can make or break the entire experience. 

### Dough Recipe Breakdown

After trying various dough recipes, I settled on a high-hydration Neapolitan dough that has transformed my homemade pizzas. The ingredients for this recipe are simple yet crucial:

- **Flour:** 500g of Caputo 00 flour (around $5 for a 1kg bag)
- **Water:** 325ml of lukewarm water
- **Yeast:** 2g of instant yeast
- **Salt:** 10g sea salt

#### The Process

1. **Mixing:** I combine the flour and salt in a bowl, dissolving the yeast in warm water before mixing them gradually. This method ensures an even distribution.
  
2. **Kneading:** A crucial step, kneading by hand for about 10 minutes helped develop gluten. The first time, I was impatient and quickly realized that under-kneaded dough doesn't provide the right structure for that perfect crust.

3. **Fermentation:** I let the dough rise for about 24 hours in the fridge, giving it a flavor that elevates the pizza.

4. **Shaping:** When forming the dough, I learned that handling the dough gently to keep the air bubbles intact guarantees a light, airy crust. One memorable Saturday evening—I vividly remember—it was my son’s birthday party. The pizza we made was not only visually stunning but also tasted phenomenal, and I proudly took all the credit, thanks to this dough recipe.
