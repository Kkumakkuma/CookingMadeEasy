Quick and Easy Pasta Recipes Ready in Under 20 Minutes

Life can be hectic, and the last thing you want is to spend hours in the kitchen after a long day. That’s where quick and easy pasta recipes come to the rescue! Pasta is not only versatile but also incredibly fast to cook, making it a perfect option for busy weeknights or impromptu gatherings. In this post, we'll dive into several delightful pasta dishes that you can whip up in under 20 minutes. Get ready to impress your family and friends with these tasty meals that are perfect for any occasion!

## Why Pasta?

Pasta is a staple in many households around the world. It's inexpensive, filling, and most importantly, it cooks quickly. Whether you prefer spaghetti, penne, or farfalle, the options are endless. Plus, pasta pairs well with a variety of toppings and sauces. When you have a few quality ingredients on hand, you can create a meal that feels gourmet in no time!

When we talk about quick and easy pasta recipes, we refer to meals that can be prepared and served in less than 20 minutes. These dishes are loaded with flavor and ensure that you never have to sacrifice taste for time.

## Essential Tips for Quick Pasta Cooking

Before we dive into the recipes, let’s cover some essential tips for speeding up your pasta cooking process.

1. **Choose Thin Pasta:** Spaghetti or angel hair pasta cook faster than thicker varieties like fettuccine or penne.
2. **Boiling Water:** Always bring your water to a rapid boil before adding pasta. It cuts down cooking time and helps prevent the noodles from becoming sticky.
3. **Prep Ingredients First:** Chop vegetables and gather all ingredients before starting to cook. This way, you can work efficiently, saving precious time.
4. **Use Fresh Ingredients:** Fresh ingredients not only elevate your dish but also add vibrant flavors to quick recipes.

## Recipe 1: Garlic Butter Shrimp Pasta

This garlic butter shrimp pasta is a delicious, restaurant-quality dish that comes together in just 15 minutes.

### Ingredients

- 8 ounces spaghetti
- 1 pound shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
- ½ cup chicken broth
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)
- Lemon wedges (for serving)

### Instructions

1. Start by boiling a pot of salted water. Cook spaghetti according to package instructions, usually around 8-10 minutes until al dente. Reserve ½ cup of pasta water, then drain the rest.
   
2. While the pasta cooks, heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the butter, and once melted, toss in the garlic. Sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant.

3. Add the shrimp to the skillet. Season with salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes, if using. Cook for 2-3 minutes on each side, until the shrimp are pink and opaque.

4. Pour in the chicken broth and bring to a simmer. Add the drained spaghetti to the skillet, tossing everything together. If the mixture seems dry, add a bit of reserved pasta water to help loosen it up.

5. Top with freshly chopped parsley and serve with lemon wedges on the side. Enjoy the zing of lemon with the rich butter and garlic flavors!

## Recipe 2: Pesto Pasta with Cherry Tomatoes

Pesto pasta is a classic that highlights fresh flavors. Add colorful cherry tomatoes for an extra burst of taste.

### Ingredients

- 12 ounces linguine or your favorite pasta
- 1 cup basil pesto (store-bought or homemade)
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- ½ cup Parmesan cheese, grated
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Olive oil (for drizzling)

### Instructions

1. Boil a large pot of salted water and cook the linguine until al dente, about 9-11 minutes.

2. While the pasta boils, heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add the halved cherry tomatoes and sauté for 2-3 minutes until they soften and release their juices.

3. Once the pasta is ready, drain it and add it directly to the skillet with the tomatoes. Stir in the pesto and toss until the pasta is evenly coated.

4. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Sprinkle with grated Parmesan cheese and drizzle a bit of olive oil over the top for extra richness.

5. Serve immediately, and relish the freshness of basil and the delightful sweetness of the tomatoes!

## Recipe 3: Creamy Tomato Basil Pasta

Perfectly creamy and comforting, this pasta dish is a family favorite that you can make in a mere 15 minutes!

### Ingredients

- 8 ounces penne pasta
- 1 can (15 ounces) crushed tomatoes
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon dried basil
- ½ teaspoon Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh basil leaves for garnish

### Instructions

1. Cook the penne pasta in a pot of boiling salted water according to package instructions, usually about 10-12 minutes.

2. While the pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant.

3. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, heavy cream, dried basil, and Italian seasoning. Simmer for about 5 minutes until heated through, stirring occasionally.

4. Drain the pasta and add it to the sauce. Toss to coat and season with salt and pepper to taste.

5. Serve with fresh basil leaves on top for a beautiful finish. The creamy sauce combined with the rich tomato flavor is pure comfort!

## Recipe 4: Lemon Zucchini Pasta

Bright, fresh, and full of flavor, this dish is perfect for summer evenings. Its lightness makes it a delightful option!

### Ingredients

- 8 ounces spaghetti or thin pasta
- 2 medium zucchinis, spiralized or thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Juice of 1 lemon
- ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh basil leaves for garnish

### Instructions

1. Cook spaghetti in boiling salted water per the package instructions until al dente.

2. In the last 2 minutes of cooking the pasta, add the spiraled zucchini to the pot to blanch them together.

3. Drain the pasta and zucchini, reserving a bit of pasta water. Set aside.

4. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add minced garlic, sautéing for about 1 minute. 

5. Add the spaghetti and zucchini to the skillet, along with lemon zest, lemon juice, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper. Gently toss to combine. If the mixture is too dry, add a splash of reserved pasta water.

6. Garnish with fresh basil leaves and serve immediately. The zesty flavor of lemon paired with the tender zucchini is pure summer bliss!
