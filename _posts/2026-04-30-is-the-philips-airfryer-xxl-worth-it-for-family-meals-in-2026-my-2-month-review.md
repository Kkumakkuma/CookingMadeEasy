## What Most Guides Get Wrong

### Air Frying is Not Just for Frying

Many guides tout air fryers as primarily for frying, but that limits their true potential. Why this matters: If you only focus on frying, you might miss out on delicious roasted vegetables, casseroles, and even baking. I never imagined I could make a delicious frittata until I tried it in my Philips Airfryer.

### The Importance of Preheating

Another common oversight in guides is the role of preheating in air frying. Skipping this step can lead to uneven cooking results. Why this matters: I learned the hard way that a prior temperature of 400°F made a significant difference. My air-fried chicken wings were a soggy mess without preheating; the next batch with preheating turned out perfectly crispy.

### Quality Over Quantity

Many consumers are too focused on the number of presets an air fryer has, but that doesn't necessarily equate to better cooking. Why this matters: I found that my cooking success relied more on understanding temperature and time adjustments than the air fryer's preset functions. A beginner user might rely too heavily on presets instead of honing their skills around recipe specifics.
