## How Does Purple Carrot Work?

Purple Carrot is a meal delivery service that specializes in plant-based meals. You select a plan that best fits your needs, whether it’s for two or four people, and typically receive three to four meal kits per week, containing all the ingredients with easy-to-follow recipes.

- **Meal Options**: Purple Carrot provides a variety of meals, including breakfast, lunch, and dinner, all plant-based.
- **Subscription Flexibility**: You can customize your box, skip weeks, or cancel at any time without penalties.
- **Cost Structure**: Each meal kit generally costs between $10 and $12 per serving, which is competitive against traditional grocery shopping for organic products.

In my experience, the convenience of receiving pre-measured ingredients saved me time and reduced food waste significantly.

## How Much Does Purple Carrot Cost in 2026?

As of 2026, Purple Carrot offers plans ranging from around $9.99 to $12.00 per serving depending on the plan size and number of meals selected. Specifically, here’s a breakdown:

1. **2-Serving Plan**: Approximately $12 per serving, which ends up being about $72 per week for three dinners.
2. **4-Serving Plan**: Roughly $10 per serving, totaling about $120 for the week.
3. **Discounts**: Promotional discounts can sometimes lower these prices significantly for first-time subscribers.

When I compared the total weekly expenditure on my traditional grocery bill—which was around $120 for the same number of meals minus the convenience—Purple Carrot offered significant savings.
