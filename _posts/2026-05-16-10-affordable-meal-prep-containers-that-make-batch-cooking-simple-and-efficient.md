## What to Look for in Affordable Meal Prep Containers?

When it comes to choosing the right meal prep containers, you want to consider several key factors. Not all containers are created equal, so here’s what I found essential:

### Material

- **Glass vs. Plastic**: Glass containers tend to be more durable and microwave-safe. On the other hand, plastic containers are often lighter and cheaper.
- **BPA-Free**: Check for BPA-free containers; health-conscious consumers often prefer them for food safety.

### Size and Shape Options

- **Versatility**: Look for a variety of sizes that work for different food types, such as salads, grains, or proteins.
- **Stackable Designs**: This feature can save valuable fridge and cabinet space.

### Seal and Durability

- **Leak-Proof Seals**: Some containers are made with silicone or special locking mechanisms to prevent spills.
- **Sturdiness**: Check for thickness and robustness to ensure they’ll withstand time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long do meal prep containers typically last?

When well cared for, durable meal prep containers can last several years. Glass containers, like the OXO Good Grips, often have a longer lifespan than plastic containers, especially those that are BPA-free, which can degrade over time.

### Is it better to use glass or plastic meal prep containers?

Glass containers are more durable and non-reactive, making them great for reheating and storage. However, BPA-free plastic containers like those from Rubbermaid are lighter and often cheaper, making them a suitable option for many.

### How much do meal prep containers cost in 2026?

Prices for meal prep containers typically range from $10 for single silicone bags to around $50 for complete glass sets. It's advisable to shop around for deals, especially on brands like OXO and Rubbermaid.

### How can I clean my meal prep containers effectively?

Most meal prep containers are dishwasher safe, which simplifies cleaning. However, for tough stains or odors—especially in plastic containers—try soaking them in lemon water or using baking soda.
