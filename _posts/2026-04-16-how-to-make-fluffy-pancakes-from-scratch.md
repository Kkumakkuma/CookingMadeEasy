## Ingredients You’ll Need

Before we dive into the cooking process, let's gather the ingredients. Here’s what you need to make fluffy pancakes:

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup buttermilk (or milk plus a little vinegar)
- 1 large egg
- 2 tablespoons melted butter (plus extra for the skillet)
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)

### Tip: Buttermilk Substitution

If you don't have buttermilk on hand, you can easily create a buttermilk substitute. Just add 1 tablespoon of white vinegar or lemon juice to a measuring cup, then fill it up with milk until you reach 1 cup. Let it sit for about 5 minutes before using it in your pancake batter.

## Step-by-Step Instructions to Make Fluffy Pancakes

### Step 1: Mix the Dry Ingredients

Start by taking a large mixing bowl and adding the dry ingredients. 

1. **Combine Flour, Sugar, and Baking Powder**: In a bowl, whisk together 1 cup of all-purpose flour, 2 tablespoons of sugar, 1 tablespoon of baking powder, 1/2 teaspoon of baking soda, and 1/4 teaspoon of salt. 
2. **Whisk Until Well Combined**: Ensure there are no lumps—this helps ensure an even texture in your pancakes.

### Step 2: Prepare the Wet Ingredients

In a separate bowl, it’s time to mix the wet ingredients.

1. **Mix Buttermilk, Egg, and Vanilla**: Add 1 cup of buttermilk, 1 large egg, and 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract if you’re using it.
2. **Melt the Butter**: Melt 2 tablespoons of butter in the microwave or a small saucepan and allow it to cool slightly. Then, stir it into the wet ingredients. 

### Step 3: Combine Wet and Dry Ingredients

Now, let's bring the two mixtures together:

1. **Make a Well in the Dry Ingredients**: Use a wooden spoon or spatula to create a well in your dry ingredients.
2. **Pour in the Wet Ingredients**: Carefully pour the wet ingredient mixture into the well of the dry ingredients.
3. **Stir Gently**: Use a spatula to gently combine the wet and dry ingredients. Be careful not to over-mix! It’s perfectly fine if there are still a few lumps.

### Step 4: Preheat Your Skillet

While you let the batter rest for about 5-10 minutes (this helps with fluffiness), preheat your skillet:

1. **Heat the Skillet**: Place a non-stick skillet or griddle over medium heat. You can test if it’s ready by sprinkling a few drops of water on it; if they sizzle and evaporate, your skillet is preheated.
2. **Add Butter**: Melt a small pat of butter in the skillet, ensuring it coats the bottom evenly.

### Step 5: Cook the Pancakes

It’s time to cook those fluffy pancakes!

1. **Pour the Batter**: Use about 1/4 cup of batter for each pancake. Pour it onto the skillet, leaving enough space between the pancakes to allow for spreading.
2. **Watch for Bubbles**: Cook until you see small bubbles forming on the surface (about 2-3 minutes).
3. **Flip Carefully**: Once the edges start to look set, gently flip the pancake using a spatula. Cook for another 1-2 minutes on the other side until golden brown.

### Step 6: Repeat and Serve

Continue the process with the remaining batter, adding a little more butter to the skillet as needed.

1. **Keep Pancakes Warm**: If you’re making a large batch, keep the cooked pancakes warm in a low oven (about 200°F) while you finish the rest.
2. **Serve with Toppings**: Once you have your stack, it’s time to add your favorite toppings. Consider fresh berries, maple syrup, whipped cream, or even chocolate chips for an extra indulgence.

## Tips for Perfect Fluffy Pancakes Every Time

- **Do Not Over-Mix**: Over-mixing the batter can result in dense pancakes. Mix until the ingredients are just combined.
- **Let the Batter Rest**: Allowing the batter to rest is key—it gives the baking powder and baking soda time to activate fully, creating lighter pancakes.
- **Temperature is Key**: If your skillet is too hot, the outsides may burn before the insides cook through. If it’s too cool, your pancakes can end up greasy.
- **Experiment with Add-Ins**: Feel free to get creative! Add fresh fruit, nuts, or even chocolate chips to the batter for added flavor and texture.

## Storing and Reheating Leftover Pancakes

If you find yourself with leftover pancakes, don’t worry! They can be easily stored for another day.

1. **Cool Completely**: Allow the pancakes to cool completely on a wire rack.
2. **Store**: Place them in an airtight container or between layers of parchment paper to prevent sticking. You can refrigerate them for up to 3 days or freeze them for up to 2 months.
3. **Reheat**: For reheating, you can place them in a toaster, microwave, or oven. If using the microwave, heat in 30-second intervals until warmed through.
