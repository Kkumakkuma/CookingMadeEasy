The Instant Pot Duo Crisp is truly worth it for anyone looking to streamline meal prep, especially families and busy professionals. However, if you’re more of a casual cook or someone with limited kitchen space, you might find a less expensive appliance more suitable.

## Favorite Meals Prepped with the Instant Pot Duo Crisp

### Family-Friendly Taco Night

One of the standout meals I prepared was Taco Night, which became a family favorite. I used the pressure cooker feature to quickly cook seasoned ground beef in about 10 minutes. Here’s how it went:

1. **Ingredients**: 1.5 lbs of ground beef, taco seasoning, diced onion, diced peppers.
2. **Preparation**: I set my Instant Pot to sauté mode; the beef cooked while I chopped the vegetables. In under 30 minutes, we had delicious, flavorful tacos ready to go.
3. **Cost**: Around $15 for about 10 servings—comparable to one takeout order!

The kids loved choosing their toppings, and I loved the quick cleanup. Using the Duo Crisp meant I could pressure cook the meat and then use the air fryer for nachos, easily crisping them while everything else was happening.

### Quick Healthy Breakfast

Breakfast used to be a rushed affair in our house, often consisting of nothing more than a granola bar. However, with the Instant Pot Duo Crisp, I began whipping up a batch of oatmeal that could serve us for several days.

- **Oatmeal Recipe**: 2 cups rolled oats, 4 cups water, and any mix-ins (like fruit or nuts).
- **Process**: Pressure cook for 4 minutes and then let it naturally release for around 10.
