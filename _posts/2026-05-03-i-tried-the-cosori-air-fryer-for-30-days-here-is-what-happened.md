## Tips for Air Fryer Success

### Preparation Makes Perfect

One critical lesson I learned during my 30-day trial is that preparation is key to achieving great results with the Cosori Air Fryer. Here's my personal approach:

1. **Preheating:** While the Cosori heats up quickly, I grew impatient waiting, so I learned to preheat it. This simple step made a noticeable difference in crispiness, especially with items like frozen fries.
   
2. **Oil Application:** I discovered that lightly coating my food with a spray oil works wonders. I switched to using an oil sprayer, like the Misto ($8.99), to control the amount of oil better—just a few pumps created a beautiful, crispy finish.

3. **Batch Cooking:** To maximize my time, I often cooked in batches. If I were making snacks for a gathering, I’d throw in a double batch of chicken wings, which would typically take around 25 minutes at 400°F. Thus, I had consistently crunchy wings for the whole crowd without having to do a second round.

### Embracing Experimentation
