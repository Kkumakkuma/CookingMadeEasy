## Understanding Compact Microwaves

Compact microwaves are small kitchen appliances designed for limited spaces, making them ideal for apartments, dorm rooms, or homes where countertop real estate is at a premium. Here are some defining features that set them apart:

### Dimensions

- Generally range from **0.5 to 1.5 cubic feet** in interior capacity.
- Typical external dimensions are often around **18 to 24 inches in width** and **10 to 14 inches in height**, which can easily fit on any counter.

### Power and Performance

- Compact microwaves usually provide a wattage range of **600 to 1000 watts**. Higher wattage can cut meal prep time significantly.
- Efficient models can evenly distribute heat, preventing hotspots and ensuring your food is warmed thoroughly.

### Control Features

- Various models come with smart controls, such as **preset cooking programs** and **auto-defrost options**, which simplify meal prep.
- Some have digital displays, while others feature simple dials or push-button controls.

## Maintenance Tips for Longevity

### Cleaning and Care for Your Compact Microwave

After a few months of using my picked microwaves, I learned some essential maintenance practices to keep them running smoothly and efficiently.

#### Daily Maintenance

1. **Wipe the Interior**: After each use, I quickly wiped down the interior with a damp cloth. This habit prevents odors from lingering and keeps it looking fresh.
   
2. **Keep the Exterior Clean**: Use stainless steel cleaner on the **Toshiba EM925A5A-SS** to maintain its shine. I made this a weekly routine.

#### Deep Cleaning

At least once a month, I recommend doing a deeper clean:

1. **Use a Lemon for Odor Removal**: I cut a lemon in half and placed it in a microwave-safe bowl with water, then microwaved it for about 5 minutes. This not only helps remove tough odors but also loosens food splatters.

2. **Clean the Turntable**: I hand-washed the turntable with soap and hot water. Cleaning it regularly ensures smooth function the next time I heat up a meal.

3. **Check the Filters**: If your microwave has filters, like the **Emerson MWG9115SB**, check them regularly. I found that replacing them as needed kept the air circulating efficiently.
