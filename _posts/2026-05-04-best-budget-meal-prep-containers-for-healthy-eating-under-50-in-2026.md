## What to Look for in Meal Prep Containers  

To make an informed purchase, consider the following criteria:

### Material  
- **Glass**: Durable and microwave-safe, but often pricy.
- **Plastic**: Lightweight and often cheaper; however, some may warp over time.
- **Silicone**: Flexible and often collapsible, which saves space.

### Size and Size Variety  
Container sizes typically range from 8 oz. to 64 oz. in capacity. It's useful to have various sizes to accommodate different types of meals.

### Seal Quality  
Check for robust sealing lids that can prevent leaks and keep food fresh longer.

## What Most Guides Get Wrong  

### Meal Prep Containers Should Match Your Meal Types  
Most articles suggest buying multi-purpose containers, but I found that having specific containers for different meals works better.  
**Why this matters:** I realized that using square containers for salads kept them fresher due to less air circulation. Using a round one for pasta with sauce meant less spillage.  

### Stop Overemphasizing Glass Containers  
Many guides tout glass containers as a must-have; however, the practicality of plastic can’t be dismissed.  
**Why this matters:** I used plastic containers on the go, and their lightweight nature made them more practical for transporting meals compared to glass, which can be heavy and breakable.  

### Brand Loyalty Isn’t Everything  
Most people believe they need to stick with popular brands, but I found hidden gems at lower price points.  
**Why this matters:** My experience with AmazonBasics and Sable proved that affordability doesn’t have to compromise functionality.

## Top Tips for Organizing Your Meal Prep Containers  

### Color-Coding Your Containers  
An organizing strategy that has worked wonders for me is color-coding my containers. I bought a variety of **Bright Kitchen** containers, which came in different colors for around $39.99 for a set of ten. Assigning specific colors for different meal types—like green for veggies, blue for grains, and red for proteins—helps speed up my meal prep process, allowing me to easily identify what’s what. 

### Using Labels for Clarity  
To further enhance organization, I invested in a roll of reusable chalkboard labels ($11.99 on Amazon). Not only do they look nice and professional, but they allow me to date my meals and write down what’s inside. This way, when I open the fridge after a long workday, I can easily spot my meals and avoid accidental spoilage.

### Combining Storage and Prep Routines  
I’ve also found it effective to combine my storage and meal prep routines. After cooking, I place finished meals directly into their designated colored containers and label them immediately. This habit has saved me time and confusion at mealtimes, ensuring that I can grab a meal without second-guessing what I have. 

By implementing these organizational strategies, I’ve not only streamlined my meal prep process but also reduced stress, which makes the whole experience much more enjoyable and efficient.
