The hustle and bustle of modern life can often leave us feeling overwhelmed, especially when it comes to preparing healthy meals. Meal prepping is a fantastic way to stay organized, eat well, and save time during the week. Today, I'm going to dive into three essential meal prep containers and tools that will make your cooking life a whole lot easier. Let's get started!

## Why Meal Prep?

Before we dive into the specifics of the tools and containers, let’s talk about why you should embrace meal prep. By preparing your meals ahead of time, you can:

- **Save Time:** Spend just a couple of hours on the weekend or your days off to make meals for the week ahead.
- **Eat Healthier:** By having healthy meals ready to go, you're less likely to reach for unhealthy fast food options.
- **Reduce Stress:** Knowing what you’re going to eat takes away the daily guesswork and allows you to focus more on enjoying your meals.
- **Save Money:** Cooking in bulk can cut down on grocery bills and help you avoid the temptation of ordering takeout.

Now that you know the benefits of meal prepping, let’s explore the essential tools and containers that will set you up for success!

## 1. The Right Meal Prep Containers

When it comes to meal prep, having the right containers is crucial. You'll want something that’s durable, versatile, and microwave-safe. Here are my top recommendations:

### Glass vs. Plastic Containers

**Glass Containers**
- **Pros:** Oven-safe, microwave-safe, stain-resistant, won’t absorb odors or colors.
- **Cons:** Heavier and more expensive than plastic.
- **Best For:** Freezing, reheating, long-term storage.

**Plastic Containers**
- **Pros:** Lightweight, usually more affordable, easy to stack.
- **Cons:** Can absorb stains and odors, not all are microwave-safe, some can warp.
- **Best For:** Short-term storage and if you need something to pack for lunches.

### Recommended Sizes

1. **Meal Prep Containers (3-4 compartments):** These are great for balanced meals with protein, veggies, and carbs. Look for containers that are around 28-32 oz.
   
2. **Small Containers (2-8 oz):** Perfect for snacks, dressings or dips, and portioning out ingredients.
   
3. **Large Glass Containers (42 oz+):** Ideal for bulk cooking or salads and dishes that need a bit more space.

### Alternatives

For those who prefer eco-friendly options, consider silicone containers or beeswax wraps for food storage. These are reusable and can help reduce plastic waste.

## 2. A Reliable Kitchen Scale

A kitchen scale may not be the first thing that comes to mind when you think of meal prep, but it's one of the most valuable tools you can have. Here’s why:

- **Precision:** Recipes often require specific measurements, especially in baking or when calculating nutritional values. A scale allows you to measure exactly what you need.
- **Portion Control:** If you're watching your intake or trying to stick to a certain diet, weighing your portions can help ensure you’re not overeating.
- **Easy Conversions:** Many recipes list ingredients in grams or ounces. A kitchen scale can easily switch between units, making it a handy tool in any kitchen.

### Tips for Using a Kitchen Scale

1. **Tare the Scale:** Before measuring, place your container on the scale and press the tare button to zero it out. This way, you only measure the food you’re adding.
   
2. **Weigh Food in Bulk:** Preparing snacks? Weigh out servings of nuts or dried fruit in advance to avoid mindless munching.
   
3. **Store Ingredients by Weight:** If you’re prepping meals for the week, keep your ingredients stored by weight to make assembling your meals even easier.

## 3. A Good Set of Meal Prep Tools

Along with containers and a scale, having a reliable set of tools can make the prepping process smoother. Here are some of my top picks:

### 1. Sharp Kitchen Knives

Investing in a few good quality kitchen knives will make chopping and dicing easier and faster:

- **Chef's Knife:** Versatile for almost all chopping, dicing, and slicing needs.
- **Paring Knife:** Great for peeling and smaller jobs.
- **Serrated Knife:** Perfect for slicing bread or cutting through ingredients with tough skins.

### 2. Cutting Boards

Choose cutting boards made from wood or plastic. It's best to have separate boards for vegetables and meats to avoid cross-contamination.

### 3. Measuring Cups and Spoons

Accurate measurements can make all the difference in your recipes. Keep a set of both dry measuring cups and liquid measuring cups handy. 

### 4. Food Processor

If you’re making a lot of sauces, dips, or even dough, a food processor can save you tons of time. They'll chop, shred, and mix ingredients in seconds. 

### 5. Instant Pot or Slow Cooker

These kitchen appliances can do wonders for meal prep. You can conveniently cook grains, proteins, and stews all in one pot! Set it in the morning and return to a delicious meal in the evening.

### 6. Blender

Whether you’re whipping up smoothies, soups, or sauces, a good-quality blender is an excellent addition to your kitchen.

## Maximizing Your Meal Prep

Now that you have the right tools and containers, let’s take a look at how to maximize your meal prep efforts with smart strategies.

### Plan Your Meals

1. **Make a Menu:** Write down the meals you're planning for the week. Include breakfasts, lunches, dinners, and snacks.
2. **Create a Shopping List:** Based on your menu, create a shopping list to avoid impulse buys and ensure you have all the ingredients you need.
3. **Consider Batch Cooking:** Prepare larger quantities of a recipe, such as soups, stews, or grains, then separate them into portions.

### Prep Efficiently

1. **Prep Ingredients:** Wash, chop, and portion your ingredients ahead of time. Keep them in labeled containers so you can grab them easily when cooking.
2. **Cook Once, Eat Twice:** When making dinner, consider making double portions, so you have leftovers for lunch or another dinner.
3. **Freeze Portions:** If you know you won’t eat a meal within a few days, freeze it! Just be sure to label everything clearly with the date.

### Variety is Key

To avoid getting bored with your meals, try to incorporate different flavors and textures. Use herbs, spices, marinades, and other flavor enhancers to keep things interesting.

- **Rotate Recipes:** Don’t eat the same thing every week. Try swapping out a protein or vegetable to create new flavors.
- **Seasonal Ingredients:** Utilize fresh, seasonal ingredients to keep your meals vibrant and flavorful.

## Conclusion

Investing in good meal prep containers and tools can transform your weekly cooking routine. With the right equipment, you’ll be amazed at how easy it is to prepare healthy, delicious meals ahead of time.

Don't hesitate to try out different containers, tools, and methods to find what works best for you. Remember, meal prepping doesn’t have to be tedious; it can be a fun, creative outlet!

So get excited, gather your meal prep containers, a kitchen scale, and those essential tools, and get ready to create some deliciously organized meals for your week ahead. Happy cooking!
