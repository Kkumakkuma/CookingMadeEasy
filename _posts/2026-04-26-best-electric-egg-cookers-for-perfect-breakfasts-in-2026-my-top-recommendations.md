## What Most Guides Get Wrong

### It’s All About Price
Many reviews suggest that the most expensive models are always the best. In my experience, while high-end egg cookers like the Cuisinart provide many features, budget-friendly options like the BLACK+DECKER EC600 can deliver similar results for less. 
**Why this matters:** There’s a misconception that more expensive always means better quality. By testing multiple price points, I discovered that you don’t have to spend a fortune to enjoy perfectly cooked eggs.

### Cooking Times Aren’t Universal
Some guides recommend standard cooking times without accounting for egg size or altitude. I found that a standard medium egg took significantly longer to cook at my higher altitude than it did for others cooking at sea level. 
**Why this matters:** Without personal experimentation, you could end up with undercooked or overcooked eggs. It’s essential to adjust your cooking time according to your specific conditions for consistent results.

### Poached Eggs Aren’t As Simple As They Seem
Many guides insist that electric egg cookers naturally ensure perfect poached eggs. My tests showed that only certain models worked effectively for poaching, while others left me with disappointing results. 
**Why this matters:** Knowing which models excel in poaching can save you money and wasted time trying different appliances. This knowledge can help you select the right cooker for your specific needs.
