## Cooking Tips for Getting the Most Out of Your Air Fryer

### Preheat for Perfect Cooking

Before getting started, I learned that preheating the air fryer makes a significant difference in cooking time and quality. For instance, when I preheated my Ninja AG301 Foodi before making air-fried Brussels sprouts, they came out much crisper compared to those cooked without preheating.

### Don’t Overcrowd the Basket

It's easy to throw a bunch of food in and hope for the best, but I found that overcrowding the basket often leads to uneven cooking. For example, during a batch of chicken wings, I separated them into two cooking sessions for better crispiness—trust me, it's worth the little extra time.

### Use Minimal Oil

While air fryers are fantastic for reducing oil intake, using a light spray can elevate texture without negating health benefits. I discovered that a quick misting of olive oil before cooking sweet potato fries improved their crispiness wonderfully.

### Experiment with Seasonings

Don't hold back on seasonings! With air frying, there's no need to drown your food in sauces or marinades. I’ve made a delicious variant of roasted vegetables with just salt, pepper, and a sprinkle of garlic powder, allowing the natural flavors to shine through.

### Regularly Clean Your Air Fryer

After several uses, make sure to clean your air fryer meticulously, especially the basket and the drip tray. I've had some unfortunate incidents where leftover oil affected the taste of freshly cooked meals. A quick handwash or placing removable parts in the dishwasher goes a long way in maintaining clean flavors.
