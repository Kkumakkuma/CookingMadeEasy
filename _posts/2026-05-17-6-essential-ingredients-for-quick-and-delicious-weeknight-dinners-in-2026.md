## How Can You Use Versatile Proteins?

Easy-to-cook proteins are a crucial element in speeding up your weeknight dinners. Options like chicken breasts, ground turkey, and canned tuna can be cooked in a flash or easily incorporated into various dishes.

### Types of Versatile Proteins

1. **Chicken Breasts**: Ideal for quick stir-fries or grilling.
2. **Ground Turkey**: Cooks fast on the stovetop and can perform in tacos, pasta dishes, or casseroles.
3. **Canned Tuna**: Perfect for salads, sandwiches, or pasta, and a great source of omega-3 fatty acids.

You can find chicken breasts for about $2.99 per pound at stores like Costco or Walmart, while ground turkey averages around $3.49. I’ve made quick meals under $15 using just 1 pound of each protein type with fresh veggies.

### Some Quick Dinner Ideas

- **Chicken Stir-Fry**: Slice chicken breasts, toss them with soy sauce, and add frozen veggies for a quick stir-fry. A meal ready in 15 minutes!
- **Turkey Tacos**: Brown ground turkey with taco seasoning, and stuff it into quick-cook tortillas with toppings. Dinner on the table in 20 minutes.
- **Tuna Pasta Salad**: Mix canned tuna, whole-grain pasta, some diced pickles, and mayo for an all-in-one meal that’s refreshing and satisfying.

## What Are Shelf-Stable Grains and How Do They Work?

Shelf-stable grains are a weeknight hero as they allow for instant meals without the worry of perishability. They are easy to prepare and pair well with many proteins and vegetables.

### Popular Shelf-Stable Grains

- **Quinoa**: Ready in about 15 minutes and provides complete protein.
- **Brown Rice**: Provides fiber and essential minerals.
- **Whole Wheat Pasta**: A great base for countless dishes and cooks in 10 minutes.

### How to Use Them

- **Quinoa Bowl**: Cook quinoa and top it with your choice of protein and veggies—ready in under 30 minutes.
- **Vegetable Fried Rice**: Utilize leftover rice and stir-fry with veggies and protein for a quick meal.
- **Pasta Primavera**: In less than 20 minutes, make a vibrant pasta dish with fresh veggies and a light sauce.

## Why Is Fresh Produce a Game-Changer?

Incorporating fresh produce can elevate your meals without adding much prep time. Stocking up on versatile veggies and herbs not only makes meals colorful but also nutritious.

### Essential Fresh Produce to Keep

- **Bell Peppers**: Great for stir-fries and salads.
- **Frozen Spinach**: Quick to add to any dish for a nutrient boost.
- **Herbs**: Fresh herbs like cilantro or parsley add an instant flavor lift.

### Meal Ideas

- **Stuffed Peppers**: Fill halved bell peppers with ground turkey and rice, bake for 30 minutes, and you have a complete meal.
- **Spinach Omelet**: Whip up an easy spinach omelet with eggs and herbs for a healthy dinner in under 15 minutes.
- **Herb-Infused Pasta**: Toss pasta with sautéed veggies and herbs for a fragrant dish that feels fancy.

## How Do Flavored Sauces Save Time?

Sauces can transform a basic dish into something delicious with minimal effort. Keeping a variety of sauces at home can save time and enhance flavors.

### Must-Have Sauces

- **Soy Sauce**: Perfect for marinades and stir-fries.
- **Pesto**: A great pasta mixer that adds depth and flavor.
- **Salsa**: Can add zest to meats or serve as a topping.

### Ways to Use Sauces

- **Quick Stir-Fry Sauce**: Combine soy sauce with a bit of honey and garlic for a delightful glaze on your proteins.
- **Pesto Chicken**: Bake chicken breasts smothered in pesto for an easy flavor upgrade.
- **Salsa Chicken Tacos**: Top grilled chicken with salsa and cheese for a quick taco night.

## Meal Prepping: The Secret to Stress-Free Weeknight Dinners

Meal prepping has become a game changer for my weekly cooking routine and might just be the solution for your busy evenings too. By dedicating a couple of hours over the weekend, I can streamline my groceries and minimize the chaos that comes with cooking during the week.

### My Meal Prepping Ritual

On Sundays, I set aside time, usually around two hours, to prepare meals for the upcoming week. With the essential ingredients I’ve shared, I create versatile prepped items that can be mixed and matched. For instance, I’ll roast a sheet pan of chicken breasts seasoned with Italian herbs ($0.50/serving), sauté a huge batch of mixed frozen veggies, and cook a large pot of brown rice ($0.30/serving). By the end of my prep session, I have an assembly line of ready-to-eat components.

I often pack meals into glass containers, which I bought from Rubbermaid for about $30 for a set of 20. Each night, I can quickly grab a container, add some fresh ingredients like avocado or spinach, and have a complete meal in just 10 minutes. This preparation not only reduces cooking time during the week but also leads to healthier choices and minimizes food waste.

### Examples of Meal Preps

1. **Chicken and Veggie Bowls**: I layer cooked brown rice, roasted chicken, and sautéed broccoli in containers. When it's time to eat, I simply heat and add a splash of soy sauce.
2. **Taco Kits**: I prepare ground turkey with spices, and store it alongside taco shells and toppings. It makes for a fun, quick build-your-own taco night.
3. **Mediterranean Chickpea Salad**: Canned chickpeas mixed with diced cucumbers, tomatoes, and a simple vinaigrette can hold in the fridge for several days, making a refreshing side dish or a light main course.
